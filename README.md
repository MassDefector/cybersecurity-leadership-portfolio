## About Me

I am a senior United States Air Force communications and cybersecurity leader with nearly 20 years of experience directing technical teams, overseeing critical IT infrastructure, managing cybersecurity initiatives, and leading organizational improvements.

My professional experience includes leading organizations of more than 60 personnel, overseeing enterprise technology operations, implementing cybersecurity policies, and coordinating complex technology modernization projects.

I hold a Master of Science in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance and maintain CompTIA SecurityX, CySA+, PenTest+, and Security+ certifications.

This portfolio documents independent projects designed to demonstrate practical cybersecurity capabilities and the application of enterprise security management principles.
